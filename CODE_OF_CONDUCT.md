# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

zskills welcomes people of different backgrounds, identities, abilities, and levels of experience. Everyone participating in project discussions, issues, reviews, and other project-managed spaces is expected to help maintain a respectful and constructive environment.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence respectfully, including when disagreeing.
- Give specific, actionable feedback about work rather than attacking a person.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, attribution, and the rights of creators whose material is used in examples.
- Accept good-faith corrections and take responsibility for harmful behavior.
- Keep technical discussions focused and make room for contributors with different experience.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, sexual harassment, deliberate humiliation, publishing private information without consent, and retaliation against someone who reports a concern are unacceptable. Repeated disruption or personal attacks are also unacceptable, including when presented as technical criticism.

## Report a concern

Contact a project maintainer privately through a channel explicitly listed on their verified profile. If no private contact route is available, open a public issue titled "Request a private conduct reporting channel" without naming the people involved or posting incident details. A maintainer should arrange a private channel before collecting the report.

The repository does not currently list a dedicated conduct-reporting address. Do not send a report to an unverified or guessed address. If a concern involves a maintainer, contact another uninvolved maintainer when one is available, or use the hosting platform's abuse-reporting process for conduct on that platform.

Share the behavior, its context, relevant evidence, and any immediate privacy or participation needs. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and limit disclosure to what is needed to assess and respond. Absolute confidentiality cannot be promised where platform rules or immediate safety needs require escalation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, request a behavior change, issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Responses should reflect the severity, context, repetition, and effect of the conduct. Where practical, explain the reason and expected change privately.

Anyone subject to an enforcement decision may request reconsideration through an available private maintainer channel. An uninvolved maintainer should review the request when possible. Reporting in good faith must not result in retaliation.

These expectations apply in project-managed spaces and when someone explicitly represents the project elsewhere. They do not give maintainers authority over unrelated private activity.
