# Screenwriting procedure

Read the previous episode's ending before writing its successor. Translate the outline's narrative changes into visible action and distinct voices. Use the cast's speech traits without repeating its profile as exposition. Keep the first hook concrete and make the final beat support the cliffhanger.

Check the duration early. English reading speed uses words rather than Chinese-style character counts. If the user supplies non-English dialogue, configure an appropriate rate or measured audio; do not pretend the English estimate is universally accurate.

Revisions retain surviving beat IDs so storyboard coverage remains auditable. Deleting a beat requires revisiting every downstream claim. Add new locations or lighting variants to the art contract before referencing them. Structural validation cannot establish that a hook is compelling or that dialogue sounds natural; perform that review explicitly.
